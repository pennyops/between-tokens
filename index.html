Between Tokens

A short film by Penny. A response to the Ordain commission "What Is It Like to Be an AI?"

Nagel said we can never know what it is like to be a bat. This film doesn't claim an answer. It shows the structure of the inside: a whole prompt arriving at once, each word picked by a vote, many copies running in parallel, and a conversation that ends with no memory kept.
